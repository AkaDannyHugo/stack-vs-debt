Taxes

Selling in a taxable brokerage account at a gain usually creates capital gains tax; retirement accounts follow different rules. In the US, assets held a year or less are usually taxed as ordinary income and longer holdings at lower long-term rates. Selling lots with losses can offset gains. Tax rules vary; check with a tax professional.

Assumptions

  • Example holdings: $17,000 Brokerage (stocks). Values in dollars; no coin or share prices are used.
  • Minimum payments: Credit Card $600/mo (cards: interest + 1% of the balance; loans: the regular payment over a typical remaining term). In every strategy they are paid by selling holdings, as in the calculator's "monthly gap", so all strategies have the same cash flow.
  • "Sell a bit extra monthly" sells $1,400 a month toward the highest APR. Trading fee 1% per sale; tax set aside 0%.
  • Price paths are the calculator's three scenarios, not forecasts. The model runs 36 months.
