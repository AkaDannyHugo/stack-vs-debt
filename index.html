Stack vs Debt: should you sell your crypto to pay off debt, or wait?

A free calculator for people who hold crypto, stocks, or cash while paying high-interest credit card or loan debt. Enter what you hold and what you owe to see your monthly interest, how long your stack lasts, the break-even price you'd need, and how selling now, waiting, or selling monthly compare in a crash, a flat market, and a rally.

No signup. Your numbers stay in your browser. Not financial advice.
