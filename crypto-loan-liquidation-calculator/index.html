Crypto-backed loan: liquidation price and APR stress test

Short answer: Liquidation price = loan balance ÷ (coins held as collateral × liquidation LTV). With 0.5 BTC as collateral, a $12,000 balance and an 80% liquidation LTV (made-up numbers), that is $12,000 ÷ (0.5 × 0.80) = $30,000. If interest is added to the loan, the balance grows and the liquidation price rises over time. The calculator below runs the numbers you enter; it does not compare, rate or recommend any lender.

The formulas

  • LTV = loan balance ÷ (coins × coin price)
  • Margin-call price = balance ÷ (coins × margin-call LTV)
  • Liquidation price = balance ÷ (coins × liquidation LTV)
  • Drop to liquidation = 1 − liquidation price ÷ today's price
  • Interest paid monthly = loan × APR ÷ 12 × months. Interest added to the loan = loan × ((1 + APR÷12)months − 1)

Common questions

How is the liquidation price of a crypto-backed loan calculated?

Divide the loan balance by the number of coins posted times the liquidation LTV. For example (made-up numbers), a $12,000 balance on 0.5 BTC with an 80% liquidation LTV gives $12,000 / (0.5 x 0.80) = $30,000.

What is the difference between a margin call and a liquidation?

A margin call is the LTV level where the loan terms ask for more collateral or a payment. Liquidation is the higher LTV level where collateral is sold to cover the loan. Each loan agreement sets its own levels, fees and grace periods.

Does interest change the liquidation price?

Yes, if interest is added to the loan instead of paid in cash. A larger balance means a higher liquidation price, so the price drop needed to reach it gets smaller over time.
