Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Short version: the calculator runs in your browser and the numbers you type are not sent to us. We use cookieless, aggregate visit counts. If you buy something, Paddle (our Merchant of Record) processes the order and shares with us only what is needed to deliver it and help you.

Who we are

This policy covers the Stack vs Debt website at https://akadannyhugo.github.io/stack-vs-debt/, run by Stack vs Debt, a sole proprietorship based in the United States. Questions: see Contact below.

Numbers you enter in the calculators

  • Everything you type (holdings, units, prices, debts, APRs, payments, monthly gap and settings) is processed in your browser. It is saved only in your own browser's local storage so the page remembers it, and it is never sent to our servers. To delete it, clear this site's data in your browser settings.
  • The site also keeps two small local flags in your browser: whether you clicked "I'd want this" for the paid report, and (for the current tab only) which worked-example page you arrived from. These stay on your device.
  • The paid Stack vs Debt PDF report is generated in your browser from those same numbers; we do not receive them.

Live prices

To show current crypto prices, your browser asks Coinbase's public price endpoint (api.coinbase.com) for the spot price of the ticker symbols in your list (for example BTC). Only the symbol is sent, not your amounts, and no account or key is used. As with any web request, Coinbase receives your IP address and browser details under its own privacy policy. You can type prices by hand instead.

Analytics

  • We use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service that sets no cookies. It counts page views and records the page, referring site, browser and operating system, screen size and approximate country. GoatCounter does not store your IP address; it uses a short-lived hash to count unique visits.
  • When you click "I'd want this" on the calculator, an anonymous event is counted (and, if you came from a worked-example page, the name of that page). No email, form values or numbers you typed are included.
  • If you use a copy of the site hosted on Vercel (a *.vercel.app address), Vercel Web Analytics also counts anonymous page views there. It is not loaded on this GitHub Pages site.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses for security and operations under the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not receive those logs.

Purchases (Paddle)

Paddle.com is the Merchant of Record for all our orders. When you buy, Paddle collects the details needed to process payment and tax, such as your name, email address, country or postcode, and payment details, under Paddle's privacy policy. Paddle shares with us the order details needed to deliver the product and provide support (such as your email address, country, the product and the order status). We never receive your full card details. We use order details only to deliver the product, handle support and refunds, and keep records required by law. We do not send marketing emails unless you opt in.

What we do not do

  • We do not set advertising or tracking cookies.
  • We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.
  • We do not ask for bank, brokerage, exchange or wallet logins.

Retention and your rights

Calculator data stays in your browser until you clear it. Order records are kept as long as needed for support, refunds and legal or tax record-keeping. Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal information; contact us and we will help, or contact Paddle about order data it holds.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13, and paid products are for adults.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest change.

Contact

Order questions: the support link in your Paddle receipt or paddle.net. Privacy or other questions: open a support request (please do not post personal or financial details there).
